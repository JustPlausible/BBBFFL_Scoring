Which workflow do I need?

Delegated lineup entry

Ordinary proxy entry on behalf of a represented team, before that position locks. Use for any still-editable position.

Missed-submission adjudication

A team never submitted at all before a trigger locked it. Resolves the round's first authoritative submission from evidenced pre-lockout intent or carry-forward -- never ordinary editing.

Locked-lineup correction

An existing authoritative submission was recorded incorrectly and must be audited-corrected after lockout. Never used for a team that simply changed its mind.

Scorer Round Centre

Calculation, DNP/Interchange decisions, manual overrides and atomic publication. The one place official results are produced.
